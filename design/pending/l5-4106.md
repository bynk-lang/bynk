## ADR: queue-delay-refined
title: A Queue send delay is a refined QueueDelay, a part second rounds towards zero, and the maximum is 24 hours
summary: QueueSend.delay takes `QueueDelay = Duration where InRange(0.seconds, 24.hours)`, checked at compile time for a literal, with the towards-zero rule #3948 settled

**Context.** `QueueSend.delay` was an `Option[Duration]` checked at run time, rounding a part second up and refusing more than 12 hours. #3948 settled that a platform floor or ceiling on a span of time is a refined `Duration` (`KvTtl`), so an out-of-range literal is a compile error, and that a part second rounds towards zero everywhere. The book said the maximum was 12 hours while #3877 and the durable events track said 24.

**Decision.** (A) `type QueueDelay = Duration where InRange(0.seconds, 24.hours)` in `bynk.cloudflare`, taken by `QueueSend.delay`, with the delay in whole seconds rounding towards zero in the binding and the unit-tier double. (B) The maximum is 24 hours: Cloudflare's Queues documentation gives `delaySeconds` (on send and on retry) a limit of 24 hours, so 12 hours was wrong in the book, the binding, the double and the type's comment, and all are corrected. (C) `RetryAfter`'s delay (#3881) follows the same towards-zero rule and the same 24-hour ceiling, but stays a `Duration` rather than a `QueueDelay`: it is a built-in variant of `QueueResult`, which cannot name a `bynk.cloudflare` type, and a delay it computes is clamped (a fault would retry with no delay) where a send's is refused. A literal `RetryAfter` delay is still checked at compile time, from 1 second as Cloudflare requires for a retry.

**Consequences.** A computed send delay needs `QueueDelay.of(d).ok()` (or `.ok()` of a `Result`-handling match), which surfaces the out-of-range case as a value instead of a run-time fault. A literal delay between 12 and 24 hours is now accepted, and a delay is truncated rather than rounded up, so `1500.milliseconds` sends 1 second. `RetryAfter`'s computed delay truncates too, so `1500.milliseconds` retries after 1 second, not 2. Hover on `QueueDelay` shows its predicate (a refined type's usual hover); signature help shows the new parameter type through the field's type; completion and semantic tokens are unchanged (no new names beyond the exported type, offered like any export).
