## ADR: map-and-set-get-the-list-treatment
title: Map and Set are as complete as methods as List became under ADR 0116
summary: Adds the missing Map and Set methods, with insertion order specified, and deprecates bynk.map and bynk.set

**Context.** A value `Map` could not remove a key (its kernel was `length`,
`keys`, `values`, `get`, `insert`), could not be walked by entry, and `bynk.map`
was thin; `Set`'s combinators were free functions in `bynk.set`, two of them
suffixed only to avoid clashing with `bynk.list`.

**Decision.** `Map` gains `remove`, `contains`, `getOrElse`, `isEmpty`,
`entries() -> List[MapEntry[K, V]]`, `filter((K, V) -> Bool)`, `mapValues`,
`fold` and the static `Map.of(entries)`; `Set` gains `union`, `intersect`,
`difference`, `isSubsetOf`, `isEmpty`, `filter`, `map` (the result must be a
`Key`, `bynk.types.not_keyable` otherwise) and `fold`. `entries()` reuses the
compiler-known, non-boundary `MapEntry`, so a value `Map` and a `store Map`'s
`.entries` read alike. The order each method keeps is specified and covered by a
fixture (a later `Map.of` entry replaces the value and keeps the first
position; `union` is `a`'s order then `b`'s new members; `filter`, `mapValues`
and `fold` follow insertion order; `Set.map` merges at the first position).
They lower to runtime helpers over both the plain and the composite-keyed
representation. `bynk.map` and `bynk.set` are deprecated, not removed: each
function delegates to its method and warns with `bynk.map.deprecated_function`
or `bynk.set.deprecated_function` and a machine-applicable fix. No `Map.update`
or `merge` is added.

**Consequences.** The deprecations are warnings-only (no `bynk check` or
`bynk build` mode fails on warnings), so there is no upgrade step and no
`breaking:` entry. `MapEntry` has no literal form, so `Map.of` is usable over
the entries of another map (`Map.of(m.entries().filter(…))`); constructing an
entry directly is left to a later proposal. The new warnings use the codes
`bynk.map.deprecated_function` and `bynk.set.deprecated_function` rather than a
shared `bynk.deprecated`, as `bynk.list.deprecated_function` does.
