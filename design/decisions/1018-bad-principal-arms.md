# 1018 — A bad-credential principal names the sum member it tests

- **Status:** Accepted (v0.752)

**Context.** `by Forged`, `by Expired` and `by Stale` present a bad credential to a route's seam. On an actor sum they always acted on the sum's first Bearer or Signature member, so a test could not present a forged OIDC token, or an expired or tampered session cookie, to a sum route and check that the arm refuses it (#4385, found while landing #3936).

**Decision.** A: on a sum the principal names the member, `by Forged(Browser)`. B: the argument rides the existing `by Name(…)` slot with no grammar change. A bad credential carries no identity, so the slot is free; it is told apart from an actor's identity (`by Browser("ada")`) by form, a bare identifier against a value expression, and the identifier is resolved against the sum's members and never evaluated or type-checked. A bad credential takes no other argument. C: the member's scheme decides what it can refuse: `Forged` any of Bearer, Signature, Oidc and SignedCookie; `Expired` any but Signature; `Stale` a Signature declaring `timestamp` and `tolerance`; a member that cannot is refused with the principal's own error (`forged_needs_signature_route`, `expired_needs_bearer_route`, `stale_needs_timestamp`), and a name outside the sum is `principal_identity_mismatch`. D: a bare principal on a sum keeps working when exactly one member can refuse it and it is a Bearer or Signature member (what it always did); when two or more could, it is `bynk.test.bad_principal_needs_arm`, since silently choosing the first is the defect being fixed. E: a named call drives a generated `sumbad` driver per route, typed and raw, that builds the bad credential of the member `__as` names and of the kind `__bad` names. `WrongIssuer`, `WrongAudience`, `WrongAlgorithm` and `NoSubject` are not offered by member on a sum: a named one is refused with `actor_no_identity`, and a bare one reaches the sum's `Oidc` member as before. A named principal that no member can refuse on a sum with an open member (`Visitor`) carries the open-sum note. `by Revoked(Arm)` drives a `sessions = true` cookie member (the fixture `3938_signed_cookie_sessions_sum` runs it); lifting the remaining single-actor limits, and a sole `Oidc` or cookie refuser taking the bare form, follow in the next increment.

**Consequences.** Existing tests that wrote a bare `by Forged` on a sum with more than one refusing member must name the member (two fixtures here did). The emitted `system` test module of a sum route gains the `sumbad` drivers. The credentials a driver builds are checked by the seam only through `401`: a driver that built nothing would also be refused, so the arm's credential shape is pinned by the emitted-test goldens rather than by a case.
