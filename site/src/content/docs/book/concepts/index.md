These pages explain. They say why a part of Bynk is designed the way it is and
what it guarantees, so the rules in the [Reference](/reference/) and the steps
in the [guides](/book/guides/) make sense. None of them asks you to do
anything; read one when you want to understand a part of the language, not to
get a task done.
